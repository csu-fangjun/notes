float16
=======

.. literalinclude:: ./code/float16.py

IEEE 754 float16
----------------

Please see `<https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Half-precision_floating-point_format>`_

- sign: 1
- exponent: 5
- significand: 10

exponent offset is 15.


bfloat16 (brain float16)
------------------------

- sign: 1
- exponent: 8
- significand: 7

exponent offset is 127.

See `<https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bfloat16_floating-point_format>`_
